Safe migrations

Change platforms. Keep everything you've built.

Migrations are where websites most often lose rankings, data and trust. We take that risk off your hands: every migration is planned in detail, rehearsed on staging and supported after launch.

  • Complete inventory of content, URLs, data and integrations
  • 301 redirects for every changed URL to protect your search rankings
  • Full rehearsal on staging, with a tested rollback plan
  • Go-live at low-traffic times, followed by 30 days of monitoring

One accountable team — from the first inventory to the final check.

How we work

  1. 1

    Understand

    We look at your current setup, goals and risks before proposing anything. You get an honest assessment, even if the answer is “keep what you have”.

  2. 2

    Plan

    A clear scope, a fixed-price quote and a timeline. For migrations, a detailed plan including redirects and a rollback strategy.

  3. 3

    Build & test

    We build in a staging environment you can review at any time. Nothing goes live until it has passed QA.

  4. 4

    Launch & support

    A calm go-live, close monitoring afterwards, and ongoing care if you want it.
